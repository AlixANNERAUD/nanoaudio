# Benchmarks

Numbers to help pick a codec and a configuration. They come from `tools/bench`, are regenerated with `python3 tools/bench/run.py`, and are only as good as the machine they ran on: compare rows with each other rather than trusting absolute values.

- Commit: `c839b3a`, 2026-10-15.
- Host: Apple M2 Pro, macOS-27.0.1 (`aarch64-apple-darwin`).
- Toolchain: rustc 1.99.0 (b940084d7 2026-09-28), wasmtime 49.0.3 (f96eabaa1 2026-10-14).
- Method: each measurement repeats the whole operation until it lasts at least 40 ms, then takes the median of 11 such runs after a warm-up run, over 3 passes averaged.
- Noise: the median interquartile range is 1.3 % of the median, and the largest difference between passes is 3 %. The load average was 3.7 on 12 cores when the run started, so treat differences under 5 % as noise.

The test signal is generated by `tools/bench/src/signal.rs`: 4 seconds of chords with harmonics and vibrato, low-passed noise and decaying noise bursts every quarter second, around -18 dBFS RMS. FLAC and MP3 files are encoded from the same signal at benchmark time, with libsndfile and LAME through `uv`.

## Fixed and floating point

On an MCU without an FPU, `Float` is emulated in software and much slower, which is why `Fixed` is the default there. The byte-slice and 24-bit paths were being optimised when these numbers were taken, so expect those rows to improve after the commit above.

FLAC needs one `i32` per sample of its largest block for every channel: pick `MAXIMUM_BLOCK_SIZE` and `MAXIMUM_CHANNELS` to match your files (most encoders use 4096 samples), and give it an input window of at least the largest frame. The MP3 input window defaults to 16 KiB and can be lowered with `Mp3Decoder::with_minimum_input`, down to the largest frame (1,441 bytes at 320 kbit/s and 44.1 kHz). The FLAC input shown is the bound computed from the test file's header.

## Embedded CPU

No embedded hardware was used, so there are no cycle counts here. The host numbers rank the codecs, but the gap between them changes on a microcontroller: MP3 decodes in floating point, emulated in software on cores without an FPU, and SBC (like FLAC on high-resolution streams) uses 64-bit products, which take several instructions on a 32-bit core, while G.711, IMA ADPCM, G.722 and QOA decoding stay in 32-bit integers. Measure on the target before committing to a codec close to your CPU budget.

## Reproducing

```sh
python3 tools/bench/run.py
```

It needs `rustup` targets `wasm32-wasip1`, `thumbv6m-none-eabi`, `thumbv7em-none-eabihf` and `riscv32imc-unknown-none-elf`, `wasmtime` for the WebAssembly rows, `uv` to encode the FLAC and MP3 test files, and on Apple silicon the `x86_64-apple-darwin` target and Rosetta 2 for the x86_64 rows. Use `--help` to skip parts. Close other applications first: the numbers move with load, thermal state and power settings.
